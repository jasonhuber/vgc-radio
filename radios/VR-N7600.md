# VGC VR-N7600

**Not yet owned or tested.** Placeholder for when it arrives.

## Why it should work already

The VR-N7600 is in the same Benshi protocol family as the VR-N76, and
[HTCommander](https://github.com/Ylianst/HTCommander) lists it alongside the
UV-Pro, GA-5WB, VR-N75, VR-N76 and VR-N7500. The app reads capacity from the
device rather than hard-coding it, so connecting should mostly just work.

## First-connect checklist

1. Put the radio into Bluetooth pairing mode from its own menu, connect at
   <https://n7wgp.com>, accept the OS pairing prompt.
2. Read the device-info line: `region_count × channel_count` is the whole
   capacity story. Record it below.
3. Press **Probe groups** and record whether group switching works — that
   question is still open on the VR-N76 too.
4. **Read radio → compare** to pull the existing channel layout before writing
   anything.

## Things likely to differ

- **DMR.** If `support_dmr` comes back true, channel records use the longer
  `RfChDMR` layout (adds `tx_color:4`, `rx_color:4`, `slot:1`, `pad:7`).
  `decodeRfCh()` currently assumes the plain 25-byte `RfCh` and **will misread
  a DMR radio** — switch on body length the way benlink's
  `channel_settings_disc()` does.
- **Capacity.** Different group/slot counts are expected; the UI already
  adapts and can be overridden.
- **Frequency range.** `READ_FREQ_RANGE` (39) exists but its body is not
  decoded. A mobile may cover ranges the 30-bit Hz field still handles fine
  (it tops out around 1073 MHz).
- **Power levels.** `tx_at_max_power` / `tx_at_med_power` are booleans; a
  mobile's actual wattage per step will differ from the handheld's.
